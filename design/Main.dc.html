“Database to pixel” — in plain words

It means when a feature is mine, all four of these are mine. Not a hand-off between four people.

01

The data

What we store and how it is shaped. I have sat in the schema arguments, written the queries, and moved whole databases to a new cloud without losing a row.

02

The service

The API in the middle, in C# or Java. The business rules live here, and so do the tests for what happens when it goes wrong — not just when it goes right.

03

The screen

React, Angular, or plain JavaScript and D3 when it needs to be drawn by hand. Desktop, tablet and phone, because dealers and traders do not all sit at a desk.

04

Getting it out

The Terraform, the Kubernetes config, the release. I have pushed ten services through to production hundreds of times. Shipping is part of the job, not someone else’s.
